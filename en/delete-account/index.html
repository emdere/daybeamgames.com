Delete your account and data

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your account and associated data in games published by Daybeam Games.

How to request deletion

  1. Open the game and find your Player ID (usually under Settings).
  2. Send an email to support@daybeamgames.com with the subject “Account deletion request”.
  3. Include the name of the game and your Player ID. If you cannot find it, tell us the Google Play account email you used to sign in.

We may ask a short follow-up question to confirm that the account belongs to you. Your account will be deleted within 30 days of confirmation, and we will reply to let you know when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your game account, player ID and nickname
  • Game progress, cloud saves, scores and settings
  • Virtual Items and in-game currency (these cannot be restored or refunded after deletion)
  • Analytics data linked to your player ID

What may be kept

  • Purchase and transaction records, which we may be required to keep for up to 10 years under tax and accounting laws
  • Your deletion request email, kept only as proof that we completed your request
  • Aggregated, anonymous statistics that can no longer identify you
Uninstalling the game does not delete your account. Data stored by Google (for example, your Google Play Games profile) is managed by Google; you can control it from your Google account settings.

For more information, see our Privacy Policy.
