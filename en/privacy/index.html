1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Daybeam Games (“Daybeam Games”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, shares and protects personal data when you play our mobile games, visit our website or contact us (together, the “Services”).

Daybeam Games is an independent game developer and is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. By using the Services, you acknowledge that your personal data will be processed as described here. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the Services.

2. Personal data we collect

Data you give us

  • Contact and support data: your email address and the content of any message you send us, for example when you ask for support or request account deletion.
  • Game profile data: a player name or nickname you choose, if the game supports it.

Data collected automatically

  • Device and technical data: device model, operating system and version, language and region settings, app version, IP address and approximate (country/city level) location derived from it.
  • Identifiers: installation IDs, the advertising ID of your device (Google Advertising ID), and user IDs created by the game or by Google Play Games Services / Firebase.
  • Gameplay and usage data: progress, levels, scores, in-game currency and items, session length, the features you use, and crash and performance logs.
  • Purchase data: details of in-app purchases (item, price, currency, time, order ID). Payments are processed by Google Play; we do not receive or store your card or payment details.
  • Advertising data: information about the ads you are shown and interact with.

Data from third parties

  • If you sign in with Google Play Games or another platform account, we receive the basic information that platform shares with us (such as a player ID and display name) according to your settings on that platform.
  • Our advertising and analytics partners may share aggregated or device-level information about ad performance and installs.

We do not collect precise GPS location, contacts, photos or microphone data.

4. Who we share your data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only as follows:

  • Service providers who process data on our behalf to run the Services:
    • Google Firebase (analytics, crash reporting, authentication, cloud storage) — privacy information
    • Google Play Games Services (sign-in, cloud save, achievements) — Google Privacy Policy
    • Google Play Billing (in-app purchases) — Google Privacy Policy
  • Advertising partners such as Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers and usage data to show and measure ads (see Section 5) — how Google uses data in ads
  • Authorities, when required by law, court order or to protect the rights, property or safety of Daybeam Games, our players or others.
  • A successor, if Daybeam Games or its games are transferred, merged or acquired; we will notify you if this changes how your data is handled.

5. Advertising

Our games may be free to play and supported by ads. Our advertising partners may use your device’s advertising ID and information about your use of the game to show you ads that are more relevant to you (“personalized ads”) and to measure ad performance.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads, using a consent message shown in the game. You can change your choice at any time from the game’s settings, if available, or by contacting us.

You can also limit personalized ads at device level: on Android, go to Settings → Google → Ads and delete or reset your advertising ID. You will still see ads, but they will be less tailored to you.

6. Your choices and controls

  • Delete your account and data: follow the steps on our Account Deletion page.
  • Ads: manage ad personalization as described in Section 5.
  • Notifications: you can turn off push notifications in your device settings.
  • Stop all collection: you can stop all data collection by uninstalling the game.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the EU/UK GDPR and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK)), you may have the right to:

  • learn whether we process your personal data and receive a copy of it;
  • request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • request deletion of your data;
  • object to or restrict certain processing, including processing for advertising;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw any consent you have given, at any time, without affecting earlier processing;
  • learn the third parties to whom your data is transferred;
  • lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority — KVKK).

To exercise these rights, email support@daybeamgames.com. We may need to verify your identity, for example by asking for your in-game player ID. We will respond within 30 days.

8. International transfers

Our service providers, including Google, may process your data on servers located outside your country, including in the United States and the European Union. Where required, these transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

9. Data retention

We keep personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy. Game account and progress data is kept while your account is active and deleted within 30 days after you request deletion. Analytics data is retained by our providers for limited periods (for example, up to 14 months in Firebase Analytics). Purchase records may be retained longer where required by tax or accounting laws.

10. Children

Our Services are not directed to children under 13 (or the higher minimum age required in your country, such as 16 in some EEA countries), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, such as encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and access controls provided by our service providers. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Third-party websites

The Services may contain links to websites or services that we do not operate, including ads. This policy does not apply to them; please review their own privacy policies.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date. If the changes are significant, we will also let you know in the game.
